Project Feasibility Intake

Enter a project, pick a deadline and team. We'll score complexity (1–5) and flag a RED ZONE if the deadline is not feasible.

Short and specific is best.
Roles are limited to FE / BE / DEVOPS, and seniority is JUNIOR / MID / SENIOR.

How it works

  1. AI assigns a complexity score (1–5) from title/description/stack.
  2. We map complexity → effort hours.
  3. We compute team capacity hours until your deadline.
  4. If required > available → RED ZONE.

Tips for judges

  • Keep inputs short: 2–4 key features is enough.
  • Try two teams against the same deadline to show the Red Zone flip.
  • Use the dashboard to show your project history.
